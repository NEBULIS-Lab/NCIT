## What was checked

- Each demo returned HTTP 200 and displayed its expected template heading.
- Stellar exposes particle canvases and animated entrances; Open and Simple expose animated sections; FinTech uses scroll-triggered entrances.
- Forty’s full-screen menu and Dimension’s intro/work panels were exercised during recording.
- Screenshots preserve original template branding and content. The gallery identifies the author and links to both the original and its licensing information.
- Clips are local H.264 recordings so reviewing motion does not rely on third-party iframe permissions. Full interaction remains available through the original-site links.

## Licensing and future adaptation

Stellar and FinTech were listed at US$49 each on the capture date and require a purchased license. No purchase has been made and no paid source code has been copied into this repository.

The Open and Simple repositories describe their free versions as GPL and also state author restrictions on redistributing the template. The gallery links to those exact terms. Their official demos are the URLs linked by the free repositories; Open’s demo currently uses “Open PRO” in its browser title, so the browser title alone should not be read as a promise of access to premium source features.

HTML5 UP explicitly permits commercial use and modification under CC BY 3.0 with design attribution.

Once a template is selected, obtain its authorized source and preserve applicable notices. Commercial template source must not be republished in this public repository contrary to its author’s terms. The current gallery distributes comparison previews only.

The chooser itself supports Chinese/English text, cost/theme filters, an accessible modal with video and screenshot tabs, device-local selection, shareable selection URLs, and clipboard export with a manual fallback. Selection is not transmitted to a server.
